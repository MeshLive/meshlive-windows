## Material Symbols icons

The Windows app draws the same vector icons as the Android app (`src/ui/android_icons.rs`, generated by `scripts/gen_android_icons.py` from the Android app's `app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_*.xml`). Apart from the app's own radio drawing, they are converted from Google's Material Symbols, Rounded style, weight 400, 24 px (https://github.com/google/material-design-icons, `symbols/web`).
